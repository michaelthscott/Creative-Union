Dear Bishop \*\*\*\*\*\*\*,

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

can ye but accept the enclosed tape-recording as genuine of my beseigéd heart, and ye shall unlock from that organ tears of beholden joy, true fluid expression of the inner light that is in everyman the very spirit of love. Readily I acknowledge myself from imperfect humility,  a hopeless case: an enemy of the adult world. As such, all too clearly a gross impertinence to beseach thee that ye love me as thyself, yet to this end my spirit implores.

</div>

With a handshake in thought,
