[Michael]

Blarney and me dad were sweet together - never once did tha shadow a shame blot the physical affection in which they held  each  other - At every opportunity vicar and verger fell in together engrossed in the task like children at play - One anomaly leads to more than another - family happiness: Summer lunch - the table on the grass by the pond - abundance of flowers - battles with insects - home grown strawberries AND raspberries - home made ice-cream - Edward Scott wiped his mouth with big white linen handkerchief - looked at me - and said: When I was your age my father said to me: Eddie when I was your age my father said to me: - WHEN I WAS YOUR AGE MY FATHER SAID TO ME! we chorused - Would you like a tree house - YES PLEASE! - "Uncle" Blarney and my dad build that day my Bedroom - The Bird's Nest - 2 grown men up a big tree and me their monkey - better'n anything on television -
