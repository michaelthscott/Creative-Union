&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I went among them preaching. Calling out the word into the abyssal multitude. Road-side taverns. Market fairs. And they turned upon me in their posessiveness and hurled rocks at my humble person held forth in witness. Verily I have wrestled with their Lord's satanity even unto the  shores of consciousness. Awakening mired in communal cess.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Vainly did I assure them of the invincibility of their evil and the humility of my mission. Our lord in anticipation of their reluctance to accept his abdication had attired his prophets with dry ironic cloak of satire. We all to a man or woman smile benovently upon the logic of our professional misfortune. Since the acceptance of his abdication by we his pure and simple friends, a new age has dawned for all creation. Henceforth all is unknown. The final weighing of the fpirit has begun. The authority of our former lord is now at large upon the land. The river of tongues seeps out on all sides, overflowing in its new abundance the doctrinal embankment within which until now all fpirit has been restrained. The lord's dictatorship is ended, his state withers and withdraws its troops from off our souls.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fear not any of thee slaves who fret in apprehension of this surge. Your property is safe. I forerun my time. These things of which I speek are fantasy. Always shall I humour you thus. Your stweardships are secure. I talk through time to thy grandchildrens' children. A change of spirit is not a change of matter, that dies as it was born to die. The spirit of god has abdicated yet his shell of matter lumbers on, machine like. A constitutional monarch. We, his friends, sympathise his fate as one such as unfortunate as I, poor prisoner of the Crown.
