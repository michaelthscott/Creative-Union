I was born and grew up in Northern Ireland, in County Fermanagh.

My parents had a small farm down on the shores of Lough Erne.

It’s quite a big lake, very beautiful, with lots of islands, small islands, with no one living on them.

It was the main thing of my childhood really, just being out there on the lake, exploring around the islands, especially in the summer.

People came down form Belfast at the weekends with their speedboats and their yachts and their caravans.

It was because of them that my father and mother stopped working the farm and turned it into a caravan site instead.

We had a small shop to cater for the people on the site and as I was growing up one of my jobs was to take care of the paperbacks, ordering them and that.

I soon got my hands on some really good books.

They started me thinking.

Didn't seem so strange, there was lots of talk at that time about freedom, civil rights, civil liberties.

Then one evening on the news, people in up in Belfast burning each other out of their homes, whole streets of them.
