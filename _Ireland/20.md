I never liked the bloody troubles.\
I'd be out on the lake and an Army helicopter would go over.\
The power and failure of the State unsettled me.\
Encroached on the beauty of the lake.\
A decision was made to develop the lake for tourism.\
My parents place was taken over and enlarged.\
They got enough to have a bungalow built and retire.\
They were very happy about it.\
I came to London.\
Besides I resented the weather.\
I was really interested in going somewhere a bit warmer.\
It would snow in August some years.
