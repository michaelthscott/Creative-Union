Mother - Artist\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(Quaker) - Emmie
         
Mother dropped dead in the garden - The spot of her death between the rockery the patio and the gravel path - when I found her it was like she had never been - an imagining soaked back into the soil - wilted flower - That's life - she knew about it in her heart - bloom and go - puppet of the ecosystem (frogspawn) - Tune called famine and plenty - The emotional jig - fluctuating lively with deadly slow - And her heart loving yet weary stopped - a train into the buffers - spirit of life soaking back into the soil - that's how she went.

To be a PROFESSIONAL artist - that was her stumbling block - society - the support of an audience - collectors and dealers - there was a housewife in her but no business woman - at heart she was simply an artist a gardener a painter of flowers - no one sought her out - she left the business world alone - money for her was her vegetable garden - summer feasts of strawberries by the pond - I'll cry if I remember her singing - Be thou my vision oh lord of my heart - my father pretending to conduct - Janet and me laughing on Granny's old swing seat - never mind.
She'd been a Quaker before she was married - used to tease father about it - saying he made to much noise in church - other times that he should stop talking when he has nothing to say - that used to irritate - sometimes - She still had her Quaker friends - sometimes they went sketching - by the Lagan - Minnowburn Beeches.

One day the Bishop was visiting Drumbog - I'd been reading The Little Flowers of St Francis - Must've been about 10 - Stripped off at the altar - I thought father would understand - he didn't - Probably ruined his career - it got in the papers - on tha telly! - when Aunt Emmie came to me rescue Emmaline Goldldman - [more - (Biog.)] not really our auntie - she was a Quaker - midwife at the Lagan Valley - It was she who had stopped the cord from strangling me - born choking - When she heard about my performance she laughed - everyone laughed - even those who maligned my father - She asked if I would like to come and strip myself naked at their meeting - She was a clever woman - I became a Quaker - Ballyparable Meeting House.
