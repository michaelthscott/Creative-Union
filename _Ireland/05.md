The year is 2968. Belfast Lough is now a Chinatown. Descendants of the Poo Pong Boat People who had been escorted there after the Buddhist takeover. Known as Chinatown to the indigenous people of Belfast, the residents of this flotilla of coracle-junks refer to their district as \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (Boat Town).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The streets are made of water and constantly change their shape. The slow politics of social realignments. An old opium granny's hand on the gunwale of her neighbours home. By the end of the day, she hopes, her family will be that much closer to the Great Pong Leader, Ah Soul.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A web of granny's hands imperceptibly pushing - pulling. In so-called secret, against each other.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The overcast nature of the climate renders the appearance of this fleet of homes morosely apathetic. Grey-brown huddled figures move slowly in drifting sampan-curraghs. The black tar hull slapping the oily water.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A wide channel cuts through (Boat Town) dividing it in two. N & S.

It was not. Wisdom once again lost out to irony.
