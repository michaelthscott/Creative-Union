<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Dear George,

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

The past, ah the past... I remember on the bus, fresh crossed from Larne to Stranrar. The boat crossing from home, and now... that moment on the bus out of Stranrar, heading out of Ulster into Europe.

</div>

The vanity of man lies in this desire to believe that because he has a spirit that that spirit is indivisible.
