[TEXT] [FRAGMENT] / trudg'd pennilefs upon the Windfwept Fhore A Prophet's Houfe is built on Fand. And ye Landed Lords of Paft o'er Prefent encaftled upon a Priviledge of Rock! Ye Harriers of Poor Innocentnefs. Naught save y'felves dost level ye but Monarch Time. And fo thus hate ye Life; for Life & Time are one. Yea, but <span class="underline">One Life-Time</span>. Curft ye are! Curft till All End of Time; Curft to thine own Next Life, thy Children. Verily they bear the Low on High! Thro' the Laboure of thine own Defendants' Greed ye burthen up Our Humane Fpirit. Fhineing allways yet; tho' Our Full Expreffive Lifetime haft ever been by Lord Coin denied. Joy-Radiant we fail midft grim ordure befpattered Fuffering. Ftar o'Love. Leveller of Parent-Love. All this Humankind as one. <u>My One Flesh</u>.

"O ye Lords! Would but ye had an <span class="underline">Ear to hear</span>, then needs muft woulds't thou gleefully liften!". So reafoned I upon the Ftrand, whilft meanwhile Night defcend'd ftill upon relentlefs Waves. Lord Hunger bid me On! Away! Not thefe Briney Roots, Bog-Berries indigeftibule, fuftain a Big-Belly'd Man wot like as I. Yon Anchourites o'Fkellig Rock team'd Brothers aplenty to affift them with their Fare. Lone Voice muft wander Mirage-Waftes o'Marketplace. Into which I went. And of mine Hunger endeavour to create an Ear-Opener Gift await All Children of All Lords; Great and Fmall.
