[TEXT] [DAMAGED FRAGMENT) / Limerick Harbour. The Fpirit of the Former Lord defended with me amongft the Loweft Folk. The moft helplefs and depriv'd Children o' The Poor. Fparkle Eyed in them God's Wrath riz up rebellious; ye Naughty Fpirits. Glee-Vengefull Levelers Supreme. All Blows their Burthen'd Weight defend from Great to Fmall; Yea as oft' a Cludgel Ftick defendeth Blow o'er Blow upon ye Parent's Back, the Hand of fame whacketh out ag'in tha Child. Humankind is weighed in the Emotional Balance of each Poor Urchin's Eyes. Wars, Deftruction, Murder, Rape, & Theft; All these fpring up from Hurt emplant'd there. I held the Victim to my Breft; Mine owne Naughty Fpirit! Percieved I then in a Vifion One River of Energie compelled to flow. God muft live! I vouched myfelf Humble Fervant of the River. And forthwith began to preach 'mongft the fchavging Mudlarks that were gathered there along the Key. Great Fplafhing /
