&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lewdnefs verily within mine own flefh drew me midft the Bafnefs of War. O Child! Waftrel Ftructures doft Thou build upon the Love of Comrades o'Armes / [BLOT] /regnant Mother difembouled upon the Hearth The Half-ripen'd Feed of her Woumbe mudspatt/ [TEAR] /orth I cryed out o'er their tumult: "Mine own flefh!/ [BURN] /ights and Dayes fpent becalmed upon a ferene expanfe of Empty Fpace enhorreur'd (alltime wanderini [BLOT] I went out of that Heartlefs England a soul-level'd man, hard set upon the Vindication of all Innocentnef/ [TEAR]
