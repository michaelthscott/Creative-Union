[Michael's handwriting]

Imperatrix Hibernia\
slept here.

Regalia of the withered leaf.

My Empire shall be at rest\
this month of sundays.

Sempiternus pluvius.

Oh great Creator of Misery!\
Dampen our spirits.\
Make us long suffering\
in this dull temple\
of stiff worship.\
Oh great Creator of Misery!\
Protect our Empress\
in her slumber.\
Long my she reign over us.
