[Michael's handwriting]

<div class="center" markdown="1">

Imperatrix Hibernia<br>
slept here.

Regalia of the withered leaf.

My Empire shall be at rest<br>
this month of sundays.

Sempiternus pluvius.

Oh great Creator of Misery!<br>
Dampen our spirits.<br>
Make us long suffering<br>
in this dull temple<br>
of stiff worship.<br>
Oh great Creator of Misery!<br>
Protect our Empress<br>
in her slumber.<br>
Long my she reign over us.
