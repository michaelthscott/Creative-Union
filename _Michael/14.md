[Michael's handwriting] When I was a child I remembers me parents telling us how lucky we were ta live in tha Empire. How in the Papist States millions of unwanted children were living in rubbish dumps, starving ta death on the streets. In those days we never thought we'd see similar conditions here on our own doorstep. We had our Troubles but never dreamt of the social violence that we see today. [Red pencil] Debt Wars. Imperial States foreclose Papist Loans. General acceptance of inhuman conditions.
