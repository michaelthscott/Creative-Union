_Janet Scott_ was for many years a  successful journalist writing under  numerous pseudonyms on a wide range of subjects most notably the visual arts.

In 1985 all this changed, whilst on holiday in Val de [horror film festival place] she met her late husband the mysterious film   director/producer Weiss.

Tragically whilst on a working honeymoon at an undisclosed location in Matombaland, Mr Weiss was brutally murdered whilst shooting. Quite what happened is  unclear, the sole survivor of the incident, Janet, suffered total  amnesia. For over a year she wandered we know not where. Her family thought her dead.

It was only through pure coincidence that she was discovered in this state working as a prostitute on the Rambla Catalunya, Barcelona. Somehow she had managed to get herself there. A fact all the more remarkable when we learn that in addition to amnesia Janet is now almost totally blind. Her rescuers being close friends of her brother Michael, informed him of their discovery and he in turn contacted  me. Together we flew out  to Barcelona on the first available plane and brought her back to London. For some months she convalesced at the family home in Ireland whilst I instructed my solicitors to investigate the whereabouts of Mr Weiss. It soon became clear that he was dead. His will made his new wife his sole beneficiary.

Time has passed now and Janet has  fully recovered her health. Her memory shows signs of returning to her, and we have hopes yet that her  eyesight may recover. There would appear to be no structural damage. In the meantime however finding herself a rich widow she is determined to resume her journalistic activity on a new basis, it is on account of this change in attitude that she now styles herself FREEJOURNALIST
