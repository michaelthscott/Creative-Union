So therefore the problem of this centre is twofold. One relates to evaluating its contents primarily with a view to maintaining the value of its contents. The second co-ordinating the relationship between the internal assessment of value and the established external conventions of value.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Thus the success or failure of the sanitation system depends on either the conformity of the internal to the external or the ability of the internal to influence the external.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This influence of the internal upon the external is proportionally more difficult to achieve as the internal value conforms less to the external.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Therefore evidence of difficulty in relation to acceptance of internal value necessitates, should success be desired, consideration of nature and mechanism of EXTERNAL VALUE CONVENTION

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Function covers this subject best.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A study of the function of value is a study of the functional nature of value.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The value of this study internally can be at any time provisionally assessed through attempting to exchange it against conventional external value systems. Praise, money, prison, etc. It is essential that this be done as frequently as possible if advance in definition is to be made. Alternatively, as sucessfully as possible achieves the same result.
