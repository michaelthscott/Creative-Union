[janet's journal]

I'm really worried about Michael, he winds himself up so much. He's like a greyhound, an oversized whippet, all nerves. Its his voice, he's like someone that talks to himself too much. Too many ideas whirling round in his head. He doesn't know what he thinks, he can think anything. Today he was ranting on about how truth is just part of the variety. Too much booze. I think its because he flatters himself. Too many biographies. He called by this afternoon to cut his hair. Cut it all off. What kind of vanity is it that makes someone think they can cut their own hair? If only I could get him to come and stay with me. The streets of London are no place for a person to live. Him and his documentary! He looked like something out of a concentration camp when he left. Poor soul. Maybe his talent is his own worst enemy. Is that what he means by "Handyman Blues"? On one hand it drags him down into the joinery and the toshing. On the other, it whisks him up into that nonsense of tape recording. What the hell am I going to do with all these monologues? I have a pile of tapes under my bed you wouldn't believe! He never numbers anything. He's kidding himself if he thinks he'll ever organise it. And if anything happens to him, what am I supposed to do with them? Donate them to the Ulster Museum? Doesn't he understand there's a balance in Culture? Just like there's supposed to be a balance in one's self. A balanced storyline. His imagination constantly outstrips his memory. He'll think he's like Vincent one day and St Francis the next. Fantasies start like little twists of breeze that swirl and turn upon the vortex till the storm has brewed and there he is raging. His head must be like an empty church. His voice up in the pulpit reverberating incessantly. He's always been like this, but what seems energy in a child becomes insanity in an adult. He wouldn't agree. Blame it all on his frustration. The hostile indifference of a reversing world. He hates television. Says its the death of everything. There's a programme on Gauguin tonight. Should be good.
