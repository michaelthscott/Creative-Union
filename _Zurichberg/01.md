Alpine vista

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dear Mike,

Having wonderful time!  Air so fresh!  Yesterday met most remarkable old man out on the piste. Goggles got covered in snow and I almost skied each side of a tree! He picked me up in his ski buggy. Mr Weiss. Bit ugly but really kind. Promised to take me out in his helicopter everyday!

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Very mysterious about himself but I think he's some kind of film producer.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hope you're well. Big kiss.

    Janet
