[Janet]

Fragments of memory

The moment when I realised what they were doing. The look on his face. Dog eat dog world. Shut up, bitch! How could I have been so blind? When I realised those people were being slaughtered in there, in the hut, I just went crazy. I remember running forward screaming STOP IT! STOP IT! and then blank.

Blind amidst damp leaves. Small delicate long-fingered hands running ALL over me. Silky furry bodies. Slender arms. They urged me to rise and led me stumbling through the leaves, their hands caressing my legs.  I had some good times with those little creatures, whoever they were. I never saw them but I visualise them as being little cat monkey people. But with neither the cat nor the monkey's character. Gentle fruit eaters of the trees.  Nocturnal. I called them My Little Finger-Tongues.  Poor  souls. I'm  sure  the  Poopoopeepee slaughtered many. A horrible commotion. Rough hands. I fainted.
