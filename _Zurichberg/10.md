&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I recognised myself! In the mirror, just now! I know why I knew this place. Weiß.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Les Palissades" a high wall close against the slope of Zurichberg. Apfelstrudelstrasse. This was our town bunker. Enough food here to last... a lifetime? What did I do? How long had I  been married to this man.  Man!  God, I just remembered. He was a beast.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;If  I  stay  here  long  enough  perhaps  I'll  remember everything... everything.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I'll just stay here. God's pet pussy cat. Porridge and cream. Sitting quiet I can... Mum! I just remembered my mother. No but she's dead. Dad, and the Green Manse. Drumbog! Michael... Oh God! Michael. Whatever happened to Michael? In a bit I'll ring home. They must think I'm dead. He's dead isn't he? The beast. Am I rich?
