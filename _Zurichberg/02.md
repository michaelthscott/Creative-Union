[Letter from Switzerland]

<div class="right" markdown="1">

? Aug '86

</div>

Dear Mike,    

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

I'm so happy! Weissy (pronounced Vicey) he really loves me. I'll never want for anything - "My little Irish Princess she will float on air."

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Perhaps in a year or two I'll buy a publishing company. Got a good name for a magazine?. Weissy's going to give me the whole world. Sometimes I think I'm dreaming. Wake up and find myself back at the grind. The gossip. The deadline. The rejection slips.

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Which reminds me. Hope you wern't too upset about the interview. I did warn you. Never mind dear. Sélavy!

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

We're off to tomorrow.  Scout out some locations. Then

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Gosh! Just realised the time! I have to rush. Publish the interview yourself.  Contact Weissy's solicitors - R. Sole & Co. - They're in the book.

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Hope the painting for the John Moores is going well. 

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Must stop. Off to the Opera - Faust.

</div>

Big kiss.

Janet
