S8. Silent (1)

Don Miguel R of frame. Wolfie L. Their profiles against the whizzing blur of railway embankment, trees, backs of the mean little houses of england, its green and brown farmers fields and hedges, and the black grey hills behind, low like the clouds. Flashes of intense autumn sunlight.

Close up towards Don Miguel, somewhat hunched and frail as usual, yet exuding an impression of tight impeccable density. As he adjusts himself in his seat, the magazine he is reading is seen to be Scientific American, an article entitled Superstrings. (Sept ~ 1986)

Wolfie meanwhile gazes wildly about. Wrinkling up his nose at the sight of the english factories in the rain. His back to the direction of travel, a childish thrill glitters in his eyes. He has removed his dark glasses the better to delight in this constant surging vision of recession.

Darkness as the train plunges into a tunnel.

S8 Silent (3)

Cross channel ferry engine. Viewpoint, looking directly down from point on ceiling. Don Miguel R of frame, Wolfie L. DM reading magazine. W writing in large notebook. Small laminate & aluminum table between them. Some paper cups. W. reaches across and taps DM on the knee with the end of his pen. DM looks up. W. passes across the open notebook. DM reads a moment, pauses, extracts a pen from his jacket pocket, pulls pensively on the left wing of his enormous moustache and begins to write. 

Presently he points to the window, just visible along the bottom edge of the screen. Packing quickly they exit top edge of screen.

DOCUMENT (4) [WOLFIE'S JOURNAL] DM's handwriting.

The essence of a journal is time. Known to some as the 4th dimension, to me it is the first.

Thus that known to some as the 1st will be the second. Though better perhaps the 1.5th for a line without thickness is no more than half a measurement.

The dimension known then to some as the 2nd remains in this re-scheme the 2nd though in fact the third.

These dimensions, the 1st, the 1.5th, and the 2nd, create what is known to some as two dimensional space. A page a picture, a sign, a symbol, a diagram, a notation. That whole business, dominated by the written word.

The fourth dimension will be that already known to some as the 3rd. It is on account of this fourth, or 3rd, dimension that everything drowns in an immensity of indifference.

When I made the keeping of this journal the pre condition of taking you on this journey it was with this engulfing insignificance in mind. Enliven yourself with your own significance.
