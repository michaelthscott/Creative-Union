Summer of 1985 I met my apprentice.\
Michael Scott.\
He is an artist.\
A daydreamer.

We met in London's Holland Park\
We became friends.

Friendship is free ingenious reciprocity.

Over many park bench conversations that summer we formed a creative union...

He agreed to become my apprentice so that the oral tradition of Anarchist barbering would continue. I agreed to become a subject for his art.

We used our eyes and found each other.\
That is what the eyes are for.

We both agreed that within a self-styled Picaresque Conceptualism was freedom's chance. Its chance of survival as an ideal.

In answer to the hunger.

So it is that I write to you.\
Half as art.\
Half to offer you an anarchist haircut.\
The first time is free of charge.

Miguel Baptista Diabrete
