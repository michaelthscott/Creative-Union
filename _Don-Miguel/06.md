Inozino. Helicopterinozino. Double diminutive. So Im a lobinozino? Whats the language with the most number of degrees of diminunition?

Science.

Aye, I spose so. You know theres a big bluebottle sitting on the front of el teu barret just gawking at me. Do you its listening to us? Its trying to look casual now cleaning its undercarriage.

Ring a bell?

Helicopter!

In a manner of speaking. Sending in sound and image to a centre. A centre bursting with information.

Which reminds me. Will you excuse me your highness while I goes and sends me waste down the sanitation system?

Soil.

Soil?

Your waste is not wasted on the soil.

Or the helicopters! They just love it. Wait wait I got a poem comming on. Open your eyes in pastures green and cows can be seen banqueting flies.

Mmm. Which brings us back to the centre.

Which centre?

The centre of... The collection point.

I got ya. If your collectin waste then you dump it. If your collectin something of value like then you have to have recognition of that in order to distribute it. If not you either burst or dump it.

Exactly. So now imagine this centre in order not tb burst nor dispise its contents devises a system where by those contents are made desireable elsewhere.

Id just dump it. In fact thats just what Im going to do. No cheating now. I know the position of every single piece on that board, and besides I'll ask that bluebottle when I come back. (mock-crazy laughter)
