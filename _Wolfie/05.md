&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;'Me perants wuz rabbets, I sppose... Course, I can emaigin it!: Littl bundle a light, plopppd outn tha heathur. But, whata I kno!; Bloody subcuntous is swimmin in yon kinda himagery. So, thats it: Maybe me perants waZ rabbets; Lotsa them on Nam’fin. "

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Isle Namanfin, Lough Erne, Co. Fermanagh, Northern Ireland.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"See, what yous gotta understan is when you dont hav ta eat, things is very diffrent. I did grow up tho! Thatsa fact. Tha rabbets got smallr. Id bin all over that littl lumpa erf. Id naver beeen resless bafor!!! Thats when I starts lookin at tha warter... An tha shors a the lake. Bigger ta rome! I thought. See, all a this time Id been daydreamin: Yon torrenta himages. An me mind ad swelld. Id see things Id first learnt in me mind! Happarations, like. First wuz a spire; Over sum trees, un a distant shore. Then, men in boats. Eventually, one starry nite, I met a man! "

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Bishop" Wolfie. Itinerant Ranter preacher, active in Ireland during the 16??'s.
