Rabbit Island Lower Lough Erne County Fermanagh Northern Ireland: Birth place of Genius O'Wolfie.

Nature is open to variety. And it is in the nature of this variety that wonders can occur. Thus was a magic child born to two rabbits. A plain unassuming pair, two of the many inhabitants of Rabbit Island. Joseph and Mary Rabbit. The child grew up there wild, alone. And, no, he didn't eat the rabbits. He didn't eat. The child was entirely magic, naturally so, for he knew no other way.

The 24th of April 1968 was the date of this miracle.
