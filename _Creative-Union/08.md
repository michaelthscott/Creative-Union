The notion of the Creative Union is thus, creative expression is vital. Growing up in the countryside outside Belfast I remember most clearly a wall stretching for miles around a local estate. This wall I was told had been built during the famine when the estate-owner had harnessed the helpless Micks, Toms and Harrys to heave the stone and build the wall. That this wall was necessary is debatable. Perhaps it was to protect the Gentleman from the hoard he harnessed. Indeed that is what the wall expressed to me at the time. A twofold solution. One may as well dig one's own grave when one's life is not one's own.
