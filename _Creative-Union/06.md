WOLFIE'S METABOLISM

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Luxophagia, the eating of light.

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

The body of light is sustained by the ingestion of light rays. Those part of the spectrum which are not digested are emitted as waste products.

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

The body is essentially speaking just an image, consisting as it does of a constant emission of waste products which take the form of the inherent character of the luxophageous being. Namely, its spirit.

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

Would it be true therefore to say that it appears to be what it is not?

</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

As yet we do not know enough about this creature. Further study is called for.

</div>
