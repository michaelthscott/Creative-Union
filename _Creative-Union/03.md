INTRODUCTION:

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
Summer in this part of the world is a succession of thunderous storms relieved all too briefly by the calm radiant days that blossom between them.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    A small party of various nationalities is seated around a circular table in almost total darkness. Their meal just finished, the light has failed. The taped music slurred to a halt.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    A power-cut.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    No candles.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    The night outside is heavy, slimy, full of rain. Hail drums manic rhythms where it falls. Overhead huge, shocking crashes rumble out before intense flashes of livid electric violet-white.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    The conversation fails. Into this silence a person speaks. Tentatively offering to tell a story.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    This person is you.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    You are the narrator.
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    And these are the elements from which you weave your tale of....your tale of....your tale of....(echo fading)
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    The narrator is y'r own face [expression]
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
/...as these last words fell from your lips in cadence of paralysing comprehension of despair, somewhere out within the depths of the house an old-fashioned chiming clock struck thirteen. Never ever had it been known to do such a thing. Nervous laughter followed by silence, mystery.../ Impossibility descended upon the whole assemblage of beating hearts and... 
</div>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">
    (The manuscript breaks off here.)
</div>
