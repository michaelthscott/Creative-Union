INTRODUCTION:

Summer in this part of the world is a sucession of thunderous storms relieved all too briefly by the calm radiant days that blossom between them.

A small party of various nationalities is seated around a circular table in almost total darkness. Their meal just finished, the light has failed. The taped music slurred to a halt.

A power-cut.

No candles.

The night outside is heavy, slimey, full of rain. Hail drums manic rhythms where it falls. Overhead huge, shocking crashes rumble out before intense flashes of livid electric violet-white.
    The conversation fails. Into this silence a person speaks. Tentatively offering to tell a story.

This person is you.

You are the narrator.

And these are the elements from which you weave your tale of....your tale of....your tale of....(echo fading)

The narrator is y'r own face [expression]

/...as these last words fell from your lips in cadence of paralysing comprehension of dispair, somewhere out within the depths of the house an old-fahioned chiming clock struck thirteen. Never ever had it been known to do such a thing. Nervous laughter followed by silence, mystery.../ Impossibility descended upon the whole assemblage of beating hearts and... 
    (The manuscript breaks off here.)
